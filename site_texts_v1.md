# Home / Landing

Craft Beer Bridging Jerusalem and Prague

Where tradition meets craft in every bottle

JEBREWSALEM is a craft beer project born from two cities. Drawing on Jerusalem's ancient spirit and Prague's brewing heritage, each beer tells a story of tradition and culture. Crafted with passion, served with history.

# About the Project

JEBREWSALEM represents a fusion of two brewing traditions separated by geography but united by respect for craft and culture.

This project explores what happens when Jerusalem's historical depth meets Prague's legendary brewing expertise. Each beer is designed to reflect a different aspect of this cultural dialogue.

We focus on quality ingredients, traditional brewing methods, and authentic storytelling. This is not mass production—it's craft beer with intention.

# Product / Beer

## Golden Gate Lager

Czech Pilsner | 4.8% ABV | 35 IBU

A classic Czech pilsner with bright golden color. Crisp, refreshing, and balanced—inspired by the historic gates of both cities.

## Stone Golem Ale

Amber Ale | 6.2% ABV | 42 IBU

A bold amber ale with caramel malt character and balanced bitterness. Named after Prague's legendary Golem—mysterious and unforgettable.

## Midnight Temple Stout

Imperial Stout | 8.5% ABV | 55 IBU

A full-bodied imperial stout with notes of dark chocolate and roasted coffee. Rich, complex, and contemplative.

## Old City Wheat

Hefeweizen | 5.4% ABV | 15 IBU

A traditional German-style wheat beer—hazy, smooth, and refreshing. Notes of banana and clove with a soft finish.
